Personal Health Record: Terms and Data Use

Last updated October 31, 2026

I, V. K. Cody Bumgardner, built this tool for one user, myself. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else should authorize it.

What it does. The tool runs on my own computer. After I log in through my health system's patient portal and authorize access, it reads my own health record and saves a copy on that computer. It writes nothing back to the health record.

What it reads. Demographics, allergies, conditions, medications, immunizations, lab results, vital signs, procedures, visits, clinical notes, imaging study records, care plans, care teams, goals, devices, coverage, social history, and records from outside organizations.

How the data is used. The tool does not send the record to anyone and does not sell it. I keep my copy for as long as I choose and decide how to use it.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time through the patient portal.

No warranty. This tool is provided as is, with no warranty of any kind.
